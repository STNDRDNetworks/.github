# Security policy

STNDRD Networks takes the security of our products, our customers' data and
this organization's code seriously. Thank you for helping keep them safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't open a public issue, discussion or pull request for a security
problem.**

Email **info@stndrdnetworks.com** with "Security" in the subject line. This is
the contact published in
[stndrdnetworks.com/.well-known/security.txt](https://stndrdnetworks.com/.well-known/security.txt).

Please include:

- what is affected (a product, a page or URL, or a repository);
- what you found and how to reproduce it;
- what an attacker could do with it, as you understand it;
- how to reach you for follow-up.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge your report and keep you informed as we investigate.
- We will tell you when the problem is fixed, and credit you if you'd like.
- Please give us reasonable time to fix the problem before you disclose it.

## Scope and conduct

Please test only against accounts and data you own, never against other
customers' data. Don't run denial-of-service, spam or social-engineering
tests, and stop as soon as you reach data that isn't yours.

This policy applies to every repository in the STNDRDNetworks organization
that doesn't have a security policy of its own.
